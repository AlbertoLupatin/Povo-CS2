Povo campus - friends playtest

A Counter-Strike 2 recreation of the Polo Ferrari campus in Povo, Trento:
Povo 1, Povo 2, the connecting bridge, classrooms, stairs and parking areas.

Early playtest: geometry and surfaces are still being refined.
Supports round-based bomb defusal with Terrorist and Counter-Terrorist starts.

Play together: subscribe, then host from Play > Workshop Maps with Open Party
enabled. Join the host through the friends list and choose opposite teams.

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with Valve or the University of Trento.
