# Library and canteen layout audit — 28 October 2026

Read-only comparison of the current source build with plans 13 (canteen local 0) and 15 (library local 0) from the 27 October walk. Method as in the [Povo 1 second pass](POVO1_LAYOUT_AUDIT_20261028.md). Sheets: [library local 0 vs plan 15](../exports/povo1_layout_audit_20261028/library0_vs_plan15.jpg) and [canteen local 0 vs plan 13](../exports/povo1_layout_audit_20261028/canteen0_vs_plan13.jpg). No geometry was changed.

The [27 October amenities survey](POVO_AMENITIES_SURVEY_20261027.md) already records its own limits: back-of-house cubicles untraced, the exterior stairs at the canteen ends not built, and no plan for library local 1. Those are not repeated here as new findings.

## Findings

1. **Both ground floors have glass on their street-side long wall, where the plans draw a solid wall below street level. High confidence for the mismatch; how it looks in game is not checked.** The canteen plan is titled *Piano primo sottostrada* (first floor below street level). It draws the street-side long wall as solid and thick, with the kitchen, changing rooms, WC and bar store against it. Plan 15 draws the library's street side as a solid wall with a continuous row of small cells, probably a buried cavity. Both plans draw glazing with mullions only on the side facing Povo 1. The generator instead builds the local x=52 side of both wings on local 0 (z 4.0–7.35) as full-height glazing with transoms (`library_0_east_*`, `canteen_0_east_*`). The street-level sidewalk (`exterior_povo1_front_walk`, z 7.42–7.6) starts exactly at x=52 along both buildings, and no box fills the space under it down to the base concrete at z 2.75. I did not check the exterior triangle meshes, so a look in game is needed to see whether players see a void through the glass.

2. **Half of the canteen ground floor is open floor where the plan has kitchen and staff rooms. Medium severity; partly known.** Plan 13 encloses a kitchen (*cucina*), two changing rooms (*spogliatoi*) with WCs, the bar store and a WC along the street side. A wall with doors separates them from the dining hall. The build has the dining/kitchen partition (local x ≈42.3, y -97 to -82) and the core, but the rest of that half is walkable open floor. The survey chose not to invent playable rooms there. For fidelity, these spaces should at least be closed off as rooms players cannot enter.

3. **The canteen's upper floor (local 1) is traced mirror-image across its long axis. High confidence.** Plan 12 puts the three exit door pairs, the main lift, the stair and a second lift on the same long side. Plan 13, one floor down, puts both its lifts against the solid street wall. Lifts stack, so plan 12's exit side is the street side, which also matches local 1 being at street level (z 7.6, the same as the sidewalk). The generator has it the other way round ([sheet](../exports/povo1_layout_audit_20261028/canteen1_vs_plan12.jpg)):
   - The three local 1 exits are on the Povo 1 side (x=36, at y ≈ -105.5, -92 and -79). Nothing is outside them: no slab, canopy or stair. Each opens onto a 3.6 m drop to the plaza.
   - The only street-side opening on local 1 is an automatic entrance bank at y -101.35 to -97.65. Plan 12 shows no door at that point; its street-side exits are hinged double doors at the three positions above.
   - The traced women's WC (x 45.6–51.9) lands on the street side. The traced stair core (x 38.5–44.3) lands on the Povo 1 side.

4. **The canteen's upper stair core does not enclose the stair. High confidence.** The real flights come from plan 13 (x 41.5–47.3, y -99.65 to -97.45) and arrive at local 1 through a slab hole at x 42.7–47.3. The traced plan 12 core walls enclose x 38.5–44.3, y -104.7 to -98.8. That puts the stair top outside its own enclosure, with a traced WC wall (y -98.75, x 44.3–48.5) running across the stairwell opening. Mirroring (finding 3) fixes the side but not the ≈4 m offset along the building. Refit the plan 12 trace using the stair and lifts as anchors.

5. **Neither building's lifts continue to the upper floor, and the canteen ground floor has no lift at all. Medium.** Plan 13 has two lift shafts by the stair; the build has only cavedio walls there. The library's lift bank walls stop at local 0, and local 1 has unbroken floor over the shaft. On canteen local 1 only a 0.4 m "lift lobby" wall fragment is built. Plan 12 also has a self-service counter with cooking positions next to the core, which is not built.

6. **The library end stair is open to the reading room on both floors. Medium-high.** Plan 15 draws the end U-stair as an enclosed core with thick walls, a door into the reading area and the external stair-side door. Generated walls near that end belong only to the storage room, the accessible-loan room and the lift bank. The flights (x 36.8–42.6, y -18.5 to -14.4) stand unenclosed in the open floor.

7. **The library's upper floor is one empty glass box. Medium; partly known.** Library local 1 has no wall boxes at all, only glazing on all four sides, reading-table seats and the two stair holes. It has no exterior doors. The survey left it empty for lack of an evacuation plan, but directory 16 does give its zones: a large yellow reading room, a blue reading room at one end, and a pair of small enclosed *salette riunione* (group-study rooms) along a long side. That is enough evidence to add the group-study rooms and the stair/lift enclosures, even if sizes stay provisional ([sheet](../exports/povo1_layout_audit_20261028/library1_slice.jpg)).

8. **Checked and consistent.** The four door positions on the Povo 1 side of the canteen ground floor match plan 13 to within about 0.5 m: the bar end, the main porch entrance (y -101.35 to -97.65), and two doors at the dining end. The ground-floor stair fire doors and the bar end match photos 24–28 of 26 October. The library local 0 façade openings match plan 15: the stair-side doors (y -21 to -16) and the façade-side exit room (y -50.7 to -48.7). The library's main entrance bank (y -62.85 to -59.15) has no matching door on plan 15's glazed side, so it rests on earlier user guidance rather than this plan. Confirm it with the user or a photo.

9. **Location of the photo 11 terrace is unconfirmed.** The terrace config sits on the street side (x 52–57), overlapping the sidewalk slab that already covers x 52–58 there. Photo 11 shows a railing along the terrace's outer edge, with treetops beyond at eye level, which suggests a drop rather than a level sidewalk. The terrace may belong on another side of the canteen. Ask the user where it is before rebuilding it.

10. **The library's local 0 partitions broadly match plan 15.** Checked: the office block, the exit room on the façade side, the central narrow stair (≈6 m on the plan, ≈5.7 m in the build), the cloakroom and corridor walls, and the study room at the end. All fall within a few tenths of a metre after the provisional fit. Two things are not reproduced: the row of cells on the street side (finding 1) and the WC/deposit cubicles at the far end.
